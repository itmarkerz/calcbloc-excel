The add-in is free. It returns estimates of take-home pay, income tax and employer cost from CalcBloc's calculators, and figures from CalcBloc's tax tables, each taken from an official government source. Coverage, assumptions and results can change as tax rules and the data change.

Bracket estimates from `CALCBLOC.TAX` for countries without a calculator apply published brackets only, without allowances, credits or surcharges. Check important figures against the official source linked in each tax table, or with a qualified adviser, before relying on them.
