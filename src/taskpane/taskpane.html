Functions

=CALCBLOC.NET("uk", 50000)
Annual take-home pay. Add a region where needed: =CALCBLOC.NET("us", 80000, "ca").
=CALCBLOC.TAX("uk", 50000)
Annual income tax. Tables-only countries use the official brackets: =CALCBLOC.TAX("poland", 120000).
=CALCBLOC.RATE("ireland", "usc", 4)
One rate or amount from the tax tables (rates as decimals).
=CALCBLOC.EMPLOYER_COST("germany", 60000)
Salary plus employer contributions and payroll costs.
